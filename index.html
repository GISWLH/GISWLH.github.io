Research研究方向

Where deep learning meets the water cycle.让深度学习遇见水循环。

Four threads run through my work, from predicting the ocean months ahead to filling gaps in satellite records.我的研究有四条主线：从提前数月预测海洋，到填补卫星观测记录的缺口。

STAGE 01 // S2S

S2S climate predictionS2S 气候预测

3D Transformer models (CAS-Canglong) for skilful sub-seasonal to seasonal global sea surface temperature forecasting.基于 3D Transformer（CAS-Canglong）的全球海温次季节—季节高技巧预测。

Advanced Science, 2026
STAGE 02 // GRACE

GRACE & terrestrial waterGRACE 与陆地水储量

Deep learning to bridge the GRACE / GRACE-FO data gap and track groundwater and drought.用深度学习填补 GRACE / GRACE-FO 数据缺口，追踪地下水与干旱。

Remote Sensing of Environment, 2024
STAGE 03 // ET

Evapotranspiration & ecohydrology蒸散发与生态水文

PML_V2 coupled carbon–water modelling, canopy conductance and its responses to heatwaves and CO₂.PML_V2 碳水耦合模型、冠层导度及其对热浪和 CO₂ 的响应。

Agric. For. Meteorol., 2023

Hybrid models that respect physical laws, aimed at extremes such as droughts and heatwaves under a changing climate.融合物理规律的混合模型，面向气候变化下的干旱、热浪等极端事件。
